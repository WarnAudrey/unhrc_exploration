COLUMNS:
--------
1. Z          - Atomic number (e.g., 35 for Bromine)
2. A          - Mass number (e.g., 74)
3. Element    - Element symbol (e.g., Br)
4. LIS        - Isomeric state level (0=ground, 1=first excited, etc.)
5. Nuclide    - Full name (e.g., Br-74, Br-74m, Br-74m2)
6. Half-life  - In human-readable units (minutes, hours, days, years)
7. Q-value    - Total decay energy in keV
8. B+ Branch  - Beta-plus emission branching ratio (%)
9. EC Branch  - Electron capture branching ratio (%)
10. Mean α    - Mean alpha particle energy (keV)
11. Mean β    - Mean beta particle energy (keV)
12. Mean γ    - Mean gamma ray energy (keV)
13. MAT       - ENDF material identifier
