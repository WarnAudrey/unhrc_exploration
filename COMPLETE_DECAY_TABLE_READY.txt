PARENT NUCLIDE INFORMATION:
  1. Z             - Atomic number (e.g., 35 for Br)
  2. A             - Mass number (e.g., 74)
  3. Elem          - Element symbol (e.g., Br)
  4. LIS           - Isomeric state level (0=ground, 1=1st excited, etc.)
  5. Nuclide       - Full name (e.g., Br-74, Br-74m)
  6. Spin          - Nuclear spin (e.g., 0.0, 4.0)
  7. Parity        - Nuclear parity (-1.0 or +1.0)
  8. Parent Ex     - Parent excitation energy in keV (scientific notation)
                     • Ground state (LIS=0): 0.0000e+00
                     • Excited state (LIS=1): 1.2442e+03 = 1244.2 keV

DECAY PROCESS INFORMATION:
  9. Half-life     - In human-readable units (minutes, hours, days, years)
 10. Decay Type    - Type of decay (EC/β+, β-, α, γ, IT, n, SF, p)
 11. Q-value       - Total decay energy in keV (scientific notation)

DAUGHTER NUCLIDE INFORMATION:
 12. Daughter      - Daughter nuclide name (e.g., Se-74 for Br-74)
                     • For EC/β+: Z decreases by 1 (Br→Se)
                     • For β-: Z increases by 1
                     • For α: Z-2, A-4
 13. RFS           - Daughter isomeric state flag (0=ground, 1=excited, etc.)

BRANCHING AND ENERGY INFORMATION:
 14. B+ Branch     - Beta-plus emission branching % (scientific notation)
 15. EC Branch     - Electron capture branching % (scientific notation)
 16. Mean α        - Mean alpha particle energy in keV (scientific notation)
 17. Mean β        - Mean beta particle energy in keV (scientific notation)
 18. Mean γ        - Mean gamma ray energy in keV (scientific notation)
 19. MAT           - ENDF material identifier
