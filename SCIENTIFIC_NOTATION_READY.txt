NOT in scientific notation (identifiers):
  • Z          - Atomic number (integer)
  • A          - Mass number (integer)
  • Element    - Element symbol (text)
  • LIS        - Isomeric state level (integer)
  • Nuclide    - Nuclide name (text, e.g., Br-74m)
  • Half-life  - Human-readable (e.g., "25.40 minutes")
  • MAT        - Material number (integer)

IN scientific notation (measurements):
  • Q-value    - Decay energy in keV (e.g., 6.9070e+03)
  • B+ Branch  - Beta-plus branching % (e.g., 9.1170e+01)
  • EC Branch  - Electron capture branching % (e.g., 8.8300e+00)
  • Mean α     - Mean alpha energy in keV (e.g., 0.0000e+00)
  • Mean β     - Mean beta energy in keV (e.g., 1.0526e+03)
  • Mean γ     - Mean gamma energy in keV (e.g., 3.4234e+03)
