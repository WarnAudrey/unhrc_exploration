1. DISCRETE TRANSITION ENERGIES (Individual lines)
   ================================================
   For Gamma Rays (STYP=0):
     • Individual gamma ray energies (e.g., 218.9, 615.1, 634.2, 634.8, ... keV)
     • Each gamma has:
       - Specific energy with uncertainty
       - Absolute intensity (photons per 100 decays)
       - Relative intensity
       - Internal conversion coefficients (ICC total, K-shell, L-shell)
   
   For Beta+ Particles (STYP=2):
     • Individual beta+ endpoint energies (e.g., 1348.4, 1397.6, 1505.0, ... keV)
     • Each beta+ transition has:
       - Endpoint energy with uncertainty
       - Average energy with uncertainty
       - Emission intensity (%)
   
   For X-rays (STYP=8):
     • Individual X-ray line energies
     • Intensities
   
   For Auger Electrons (STYP=9):
     • Individual Auger electron energies
     • Intensities

2. MEAN ENERGIES (Summary statistics)
   ===================================
   • Mean alpha energy (average over all α emissions)
   • Mean beta energy (average over all β emissions)
   • Mean gamma energy (average over all γ emissions)
   
   → These are what's currently shown in your summary table!

3. Q-VALUE (Total decay energy)
   ============================
   • Total energy released in decay
   • Already shown in your table

4. EXCITATION ENERGIES
   ===================
   • Parent excitation energy (for excited states like Br-74m)
   • Daughter excitation energies (if decay goes to excited state)
   • Already shown in your table (Parent Ex column)

5. CONTINUOUS SPECTRA (Energy distributions)
   =========================================
   • Beta continuum (energy distribution function)
   • Stored as tabulated probability vs energy
